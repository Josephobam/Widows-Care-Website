A decade of impact

WICCO @ 10

Widows Care celebrating a decade of impactful service

Since 2016, Widows Care and Charity has continued to stand with widows and their families through compassionate care, practical support, and community action. The 10-year milestone reflects a decade of service shaped by dignity, hope, and practical help.

From food distribution and community outreach to medical support and empowerment programmes, the organisation has kept faith with its mission of restoring hope and strengthening vulnerable families.

10 Years A Decade of Impact Celebrating 10 Years of Service

  • Y2026 Feed the Widows — a major food support activity for the anniversary year.
  • Medical Outreach — one of the organisation's practical care initiatives.

Our Vision

To create a world where every widow is empowered, self-reliant, and treated with utmost dignity and social justice.

Our Mission

To provide sustainable economic opportunities, comprehensive healthcare support, and advocacy for widows and their children.

Y2026 Feed the Widows

One of the major activities connected to WICCO @ 10

Food support has remained central to Widows Care's care model. Y2026 Feed the Widows reflects the organisation's practical and compassionate response to the needs of widows and family members.

As part of the 10-year anniversary, this outreach brings together care, fellowship, and support in a way that continues the organisation's mission with warmth and dignity.

  • Food distribution and care packages
  • Community solidarity and encouragement
  • Support for widows and families

Medical Outreach

Healthcare support remains part of our care for families

Widows Care continues to see health and wellbeing as a core part of dignity and restoration. Medical outreach has been and remains one of the practical ways the organisation demonstrates care.

Through outreach and community engagement, the organisation supports widows and families with practical encouragement and access to information and care pathways that strengthen everyday wellbeing.

  • Community-focused health support
  • Relevant health outreach and education
  • Continued care for widows and families
